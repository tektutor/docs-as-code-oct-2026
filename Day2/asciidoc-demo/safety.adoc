== Safety document

WARNING: The battery pack stores enough energy to cause burns and fire.
Only a trained technician may open it.

Before you work on the pack:

. Switch off the scooter and remove the key.
. Wait {wait-minutes} minutes, so the capacitors in the motor controller discharge.
. Measure the pack voltage at the main connector.
It must be below 60 V before you touch any part inside the pack.
. Wear insulated gloves rated for at least 1000 V.

[[safety-faults]]
=== Faults that need a technician

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Fault code | What to do 
| F012 | Cell over-voltage. Do not charge. Read the cell voltages with the diagnostic tool.
| F013 | Cell under-voltage. Charge slowly at 1 A, then read the cell voltages agin.
|===
